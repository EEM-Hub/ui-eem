**Information architecture** is the structural design of shared [information](./Information) environments, in particular the organisation of [websites](./Website) and [software](./Software) to support [usability](./Usability) and findability.

 

The term information architecture was coined by [Richard Saul Wurman](./Richard_Saul_Wurman).[[1]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-1) Since its inception, information architecture has become an emerging [community of practice](./Community_of_practice) focused on applying principles of [design](./Design), [architecture](./Architecture) and [information science](./Information_science) in digital spaces.[[2]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-What-2)

 

Typically, a [model](./Scientific_modelling) or [concept](./Concept) of [information](./Information) is used and applied to activities which require explicit details of complex [information systems](./Information_system). These activities include [library](./Library) systems and [database](./Database) development.[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed)*]

 

## Definition

 

The term *information architecture* has different meanings in different branches of [information systems](./Information_system) or [information technology](./Information_technology).[[3]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-:0-3)

 

### User experience

 

In [user experience design](./User_experience_design), information architecture has been described as the  structural design of shared information environments,[[4]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMorvilleRosenfeld2007-4): 4  comprising the study and practice of organising and labelling web sites, intranets, online communities, and software to support [user experience](./User_experience), in particular, the [findability](./Findability) and [usability](./Usability) of information.[[2]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-What-2)[[5]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-5)[[6]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-6) It has also been described as an emerging [community of practice](./Community_of_practice) focused on bringing principles of design and architecture to the digital landscape.[[4]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMorvilleRosenfeld2007-4): 4 [[7]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-7)

 

### Information systems

 

Technically speaking, information architecture comprises the combination of organization, labeling, search and navigation systems within websites and intranets,[[4]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMorvilleRosenfeld2007-4): 4  serving as a navigational aid to the content of information-rich systems.[[8]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-8)

 

### Data architecture

 

Information architecture can be described as a subset of [data architecture](./Data_architecture) where usable data is constructed, designed, and arranged in a fashion most useful to the users of data.[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed)*]

 

### Systems design

 

In the field of [systems design](./Systems_design), for example, information architecture is a component of [enterprise architecture](./Enterprise_architecture) that deals with the information component when describing the structure of an enterprise.[[3]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-:0-3) Some system design practitioners regard information architecture as strictly the application of [information science](./Information_science) to [web design](./Web_design), which considers such issues as [classification](./Classification) and [information retrieval](./Information_retrieval), and not factors like [user experience](./User_experience) and [information design](./Information_design).[[3]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-:0-3)

 

## Principles

 

Principles of information architecture include the following:[[9]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-9)[[10]](./Information_architecture#cite_note-10)

 
- The principle of objects
- The principle of choices
- The principle of disclosure
- The principle of exemplars
- The principle of front doors
- The principle of multiple classification
- The principle of focused navigation
- The principle of growth

 

## History

 

[Richard Saul Wurman](./Richard_Saul_Wurman) is credited with coining the term information architecture in relation to the design of information.[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed)*]

 

From 1998 to 2015, [Peter Morville](./Peter_Morville) and [Louis Rosenfeld](./Louis_Rosenfeld) were co-authors of *Information Architecture for the World Wide Web.*[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed)*]

 

Other authors include [Jesse James Garrett](./Jesse_James_Garrett) and [Christina Wodtke](./Christina_Wodtke).[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed)*]
